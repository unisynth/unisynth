Converted songs

383 songs converted to the 64k project's own synthesizer. Your browser plays each one live with that synthesizer (WebAssembly), from a song file of a few kilobytes; there are no audio recordings here. Each is an adapted version, not the original recording, and sounds close to but not identical with it. No licence is stated for the released songs: the music belongs to its authors. They are published here while the authors are asked for permission, and a song is removed at any author's request.
