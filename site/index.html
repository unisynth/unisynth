01the voice

Fixed slots. The song decides what fills them.

A voice is 3 oscillators, a noise source, 4 filters, 4 envelopes, 2 LFOs and a 16-slot mod matrix. A WaveSabre bass and a Tunefish pad are both just values in those slots. Control runs on an absolute 32-frame grid that tightens to 8 frames for 256 frames after every note edge.

02device rack

What is in the box

Every panel is a module a build can include or leave out. The 64k player links only the ones its music touches.

osc · seven types

One oscillator slot, seven engines

Phase-mod operator, band-limited saw and pulse, spectral wavetable with 1–10 unison layers, a sampler for GSM and GM.DLS, V2's naive oscillator, the 1997 cauldron waveforms, and the detuned oscillator pair of a 4k synth.

3 slotschained by phase modulation
filt · four lineages

Filters that sleep when open

State-variable, ladder, RBJ, resonator notch, V2's feedback SVF and 4-pole. Filters 1 and 2 run single, serial or parallel. An open filter is not run at all.

1–6 %less time with open filters
mod · matrix

Envelopes, LFOs, 16 routes

Each envelope keeps its original curve law. Matrix routes are source, target, amount and operation: scale, offset or add.

16 slotsany parameter is a target
convert · five formats

Five families in, one format out

The converter reads WaveSabre, Tunefish 4, Farbrausch V2, mini-cauldron and 4k-synth music. A failed conversion writes nothing at all.

359/359V2 files loaded byte-identical
threads · opt-in

Same bits, any block size

Output does not depend on how a render is split. Threads are opt-in and bit-identical. A build without them has no thread code at all.

1.7–4.5×faster at 8 workers
speech · channel strip

It talks

V2's formant speech synth with 69 phonemes, and its channel strip: DC filter, look-ahead compressor, 150 Hz bass boost, drive, chorus.

69phonemes
daw · in the browser

A DAW that runs the intro's own code

Arrangement, piano roll, generated device panels, mixer and bounce, compiled from the same Rust to WebAssembly. Live playback is bit-identical from frame 0. A live view draws each voice on its envelope, the LFO phases and the filter spectra.

0.1 msper 128 frames, 24 % of budget worst case

4k · Mentor's synth

A 4k intro's synth, as settings

Mentor wrote the synth of TBC's 4k intros Atrium (Breakpoint 2008) and Elevated (Breakpoint 2009), both with music by Puryx. It is now a set of settings in the same voice: a detuned oscillator pair, a pitch drop for the kicks, a linear envelope with a hold, and a resonant filter swept by two LFOs. Its tree of filter, delay, distortion and mixer machines becomes tracks, effect devices and mix buses. Both soundtracks are on the songs page.

2/2soundtracks within 0.14 dB of the original code
03fx 65–73

Fifteen effects in, nine kinds out

Nothing was rewritten to sound "close enough". Each legacy algorithm survives whole as a mode of its kind, with its own parameters in their original order. At 44.1 kHz and tempo 120 every mode renders bit-identically to the effect it came from.

Left: WaveSabre in orange, Tunefish 4 in cyan, Screwdriver from the qm210 WaveSabre fork in green. Right: V2, cauldron and 4k-synth modes joined the same kinds later.

04size budget

Pay only for what the music plays

Every module is a cargo feature. The converter reads the music and prints the features it needs. Leave a module out and anything that uses it fails at load with UnsupportedModule, never silently.

06how it was built

One week, as a piano roll

Every step was a written change first: proposal, design, task list, then code. Sixteen synth changes landed between 25 and 31 October 2026. Solid notes are done, dashed ones are still open. Scroll to play.

  1. Port first, then unify

    WaveSabre and Tunefish 4 were ported to Rust and checked against upstream before anything was merged. The Tunefish port passed on the first run.

  2. Old engines as oracles

    The legacy engines stay in the tree as judges until a parity report passes and an A/B listen is signed off. Where no port exists, the original code is the judge: Mentor's 4k synth is assembled from its source and run under Wine.

  3. Measurements redesign it

    The first full run found mono noise 3–5 dB low, 2 ms envelopes 1.75 dB quiet, voices that would not die and a matrix scan eating a quarter of the time. Each became a fix.

  4. Reverse-engineer what shipped

    Two 1997 DOS intros by The Black Lotus ran under an x86 emulator until their music started. The rebuilt generator reproduces every sample byte for byte: 32 of 32, 66 of 66.

  5. Measure size, never guess

    Bare i686 probes, packed with squishy and xz, in six compile configurations. Per-module cost and ablation builds. Ideas that made the player bigger are written down too.

  6. Ask the musicians

    Released music is published only as credited, adapted versions while its authors are asked, and comes down at their request. Teo said yes first. Original recordings and engine sources stay off the repository.

09greetings

Built on your shoulders

unisynth is a unification, not an invention. Every sound it makes was first made by someone else. These are the teams whose work it learned from, and a word to the coders and musicians in them.

WaveSabre

Logicoma

MIT · github.com/logicomacorp/WaveSabre

The phase-mod and subtractive voices, the GSM and GM.DLS samplers, eight effects, and the track, receive and sidechain model the whole player still uses.

to the codersYou open-sourced a full 64k synth with its tooling. We ported it line by line before touching anything, and your algorithms are still inside, bit for bit, as modes.

to the musiciansThe tracks written on WaveSabre were our test suite. Every parity number on this page was earned against your patches.

Tunefish 4

Brain Control

GPL-3.0 upstream · github.com/paynebc/tunefish

The spectral wavetable oscillator with unison layers, the rate envelopes, seven effect modes and the .tfm format.

to the codersThe wavetable oscillator is the most musical slot in the voice, and it is yours. Your engine was clear enough that our port matched on the first run.

to the musiciansYour patches are the reason the size table still has a yellow row. We are not done making your music smaller.

V2

Farbrausch

github.com/farbrausch/fr_public · v2redux as reference

The naive oscillator, feedback SVF and 4-pole filters, drive, frame envelopes and LFOs, the channel strip, the comb reverb, the look-ahead compressor, and Ronan, the speech synth.

to the codersV2 set the standard a generation of 64k synths measured itself against. Releasing the source let us learn it properly instead of guessing.

to the musiciansFifteen of your tracks pass parity. Ronan still talks.

mini-cauldron · 1997

The Black Lotus

analysis only · no source was available

The cauldron oscillator, the flanger comb and the wrap distortion, recovered from two DOS intros by running them in an emulator until the music started.

to the codersAbout 1.4 KB of x87 code made a whole soundtrack. We needed an emulator, a disassembler and a week to read it. Respect.

to the musiciansYour phrases are now playable instruments in a browser DAW, nearly thirty years later.

Screwdriver

Team210 / QM

qm210's WaveSabre fork

The curve distortion, now mode 1 of distortion.

to qmA fork that adds one good device is how a scene tool grows. It is in.

4k synth · 2008–09

TBC, with Rgba and Loonies

Elevated source release · CC BY-NC-SA 3.0

Mentor's oscillator pair, pitch drop and linear envelope, the LFO-swept filter, the atan, sine and knee distortions, and the ping-pong and allpass delays. Learned from the Elevated source release and checked against it; no code copied.

to MentorA whole synth, filter tree included, in about 400 lines of x87, small enough to share a 4k with iq's landscapes. Running your code as the judge was a pleasure.

to PuryxYour two soundtracks were the test: every number for this synth was earned against them, and both now play in a browser DAW.
