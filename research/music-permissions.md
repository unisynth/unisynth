# Music permissions

The songs in `project/music/released/` (no licence; see `project/music/NOTICE.md`) are published while their authors
are asked. This page holds the request template and every composer to ask, generated from each song's `meta.author`
(2026-11-09). Teo already gave permission (his songs are not listed) and helps reach the other musicians.

## Request template

```text
Subject: unisynth: may we publish your music from <PRODUCTION>?

Hi <NAME>,

I'm robson of Exceed. I'm building unisynth (https://unisynth.exceed.hu), an open-source (MIT) software synth for 64k
intros that unifies WaveSabre, Tunefish, V2 and other scene synths in one engine, with a browser DAW.

To test it against real music, I converted released scene songs to it, including yours:

<SONG LIST: title, production, year, link on the songs page>

The songs are adapted versions, not the original recordings: the song data was read from the release (or from
modland) and is played by unisynth's equivalent of the original synth. Each page credits you, links the production,
says it is an adapted version and states that the music belongs to you. Listen here: https://unisynth.exceed.hu/songs/

May we keep publishing them on the songs page and in the DAW as adapted versions, credited to you?
- Yes: nothing changes, and I note your permission in the repository's NOTICE.md (with the credit you prefer).
- Yes, with conditions: tell me (a different credit, a link, only some of the songs …).
- No: I remove them right away, from the site and from the repository.

Thanks for the music, and for making it in a 64k synth in the first place.

robson / Exceed
```
