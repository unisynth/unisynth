# src/id21.rs (sidecar: full detail of the AGENTS.md row)

Pull-only (not auto-injected).

- `Id21::new(&[f32;13])`, `set_param(i, v)` (atomic: `Err(Index)` i ≥ 13, `Err(Range)` non-finite / outside [0,1], `Err(Partials)` when `(trunc(100·P4)+1)·(trunc(100·P6)+1)` > `MAX_PARTIALS` = 512; judged on the whole resulting vector), `note_on(note, offset)` / `note_off(note, offset)` (`Err(Note)` > 127, `Err(Offset)`, `Err(QueueFull)` at 256), `process(l, r)` **overwrites** (equal lengths, panics otherwise), `active_voices()`, `voice_notes()`, `check(p)`.
- Additive bank of undriven two-pole sine resonators per voice, **everything latched at note-on** (automation reaches the next note only): P0..P3 ADSR (`Adsr`: level persists across notes, release from the held level, voice freed on the sample its tick returns stage 0 and renders nothing after it), P4/P6 partial counts, P5 ratio spread, P7 = bit pattern of the float is the hash seed of every draw (**one ulp changes every partial**), P8 detune, P9 spectral slope, P10 ratio snapping, P11 gain, P12 pan spread; velocity does not exist.
- Shared `synth::Synth` scheduler.
- Math: f32 table sine (`id34::sin_with`), f64 `libm` exp/log/exp2 rounded to f32; **tolerance parity, not bit parity** (song dev 5 99.45 % exact, max 9.4e-4 at peak 3.64; dev 6 99.9998 %, 1.2e-7; the vec04 outlier = one-ulp pow rounding of a partial frequency amplified by 512 high partials).
- Not modelled: mono/legato, unison, slide, more than 512 partials (the original allows 10,201), other rates.
- **Standalone poly-only API: cannot accept mono/unison and cannot detect them; the adapter refuses any chunk longer than 13 params + size.** See `research/glorp-devices.md` §ID 21.
