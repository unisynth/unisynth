# src/id20.rs (sidecar: full detail of the AGENTS.md row)

Pull-only (not auto-injected).

- `Id20::new(&[f32;16])`, `set_param(i, v)` (atomic: `Err(Index)` i ≥ 16, `Err(Range)` non-finite / outside [0,1]), `note_on(note, velocity, offset)` / `note_off(note, offset)` (`Err(Note)` > 127, `Err(Velocity)` > `MAX_VELOCITY` = 213, `Err(Offset)` > `i32::MAX`, `Err(QueueFull)` at 256 queued; **note-off is inaudible** but takes a slot), `process(l, r)` **overwrites** (instrument; equal lengths, panics otherwise), `active_voices()`, `voice_notes()`, `check(p)`, `noise_decay_exponent(p13, vel)`.
- **The `process` call length is part of the model** (expiry is judged at sub-block ends, which include the call end; player / reference = 128; other sizes self-consistent, not reference-equal); lengths differ or > `i32::MAX` panic before any write.
- Twelve negative-feedback comb lines (3 rendered groups of 4; the 4th group and so **P4 are inert**) excited by high/low-passed noise × envelope `e^((P13+0.1·v)/735 − 1/630)` plus a damped click sine, `v = vel/128`; each group sum through the ID 34 Hilbert shifter (`pub(crate)` `Section`/`Hist`/`tick`/`sin_with`), the three halved, then a master shifter (`4000·P15 − 2000` Hz).
- **All 16 params latched at note-on; the render loop reads none.** The note + `trunc(150·P0)` seed a hash chain (`id25::hash`) that keeps drawing noise in `process`.
- Delay lines are 128-sample rings with the original's 44100-entry write index / smoothed time bookkeeping; **delay contents, time, write index, shifter state, noise filters and the level follower persist across notes of a voice** (nothing cleared at note-on).
- **A voice ends when its level follower (rise 0.101, fall ≈ 0.99999−0.0364) is < 1e-4 at the end of a sub-block**; the follower starts at 0, so a note whose first sub-block is a few samples long is freed unheard (`kill_*` vectors).
- Shared scheduler with velocity (`Synth::note_on_vel`).
- **Velocity ceiling 213**: above it with P13 = 1 the noise grows without bound; refused here, by the song preflight (per track) and latched in the adapter.
- Math: f64 `libm` `exp`/`sin`/`cos` rounded to f32 (`id25::expf`), table sine.
- Whole song device 4 98.80 % exact, max 1.12e-4 at peak 4.73 (the C library's `exp` ulp); 53 vectors bit-exact.
- Poly-only, 44.1 kHz; **cannot detect mono/unison: the adapter refuses a chunk longer than 16 params + size.** See `research/glorp-devices.md` §ID 20.
